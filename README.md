# Netflix-Data-Analysis-Tableau-Dashboard
Developed an interactive Tableau dashboard to analyze Netflix's movies and TV shows, exploring geographical distribution, content ratings, genre popularity, release-year trends, and content additions through dynamic filters and data visualizations.

**1. Project Overview**

This project focuses on analyzing and visualizing Netflix's content catalogue using Tableau. The objective is to explore the distribution, characteristics, and trends of movies and television shows available on Netflix through an interactive and visually informative dashboard.

The project uses Netflix catalogue data to identify meaningful insights related to content types, geographical distribution, audience ratings, popular genres, release years, and content addition patterns.

An interactive Tableau dashboard was developed to bring these insights together in a single view, allowing users to explore the Netflix catalogue through dynamic filters and multiple visualization techniques.

The dashboard combines geographical maps, bar charts, area charts, bubble charts, and interactive information panels to provide a comprehensive overview of Netflix's content library.

**2. Project Objectives**

The major objectives of this project are:

-Analyze the distribution of movies and TV shows available on Netflix.
-Understand the geographical distribution of Netflix content across different countries.
-Identify the most common content ratings and classification categories.
-Explore the most frequently represented genres on the platform.
-Analyze content addition trends over different years.
-Examine the relationship between release years and content availability.
-Develop an interactive dashboard for exploring individual titles and their attributes.
-Transform raw data into meaningful visual insights to support data-driven interpretation.

**3. Dataset Description**

The project uses a Netflix titles dataset containing information about movies and television shows.
The dataset includes the following attributes:

Attribute                  	Description

**4. Dashboard Components & Analysis**

The dashboard consists of several visualizations designed to analyze different aspects of Netflix's content catalogue.

**A. Interactive Content Selection and Information Panel**

The dashboard provides interactive dropdown filters that allow users to select:
-Content Type: Movie or TV Show.
-Title: Specific movie or television show.

Based on the selected title, the information panel dynamically displays:
-Content rating.
-Release year.
-Duration.
-Date added to Netflix.
-Genre classification.
-Description of the selected title.
This functionality allows users to explore individual titles without navigating through the original dataset.

**B. Geographical Analysis – Total Movies & TV Shows by Country**

A geographical map visualization is used to examine the distribution of Netflix content across different countries.
**Key aspects:**
-Displays the geographical distribution of titles.
-Uses color intensity to represent the number of titles associated with different countries.
-Helps identify countries with higher and lower content representation.
-Provides a geographical perspective on Netflix's content catalogue.
This visualization makes it easier to understand the international distribution of Netflix content.

A bubble chart illustrates the proportion of movies and television shows in the Netflix catalogue.

Key analysis:
-Compares the total number of movies and television shows.
-Represents their relative proportions visually.
-Highlights the larger representation of movies in the analyzed dataset.

-Stand-Up Comedy.

Key analysis:
-Identifies the most frequently represented genre combinations.
-Compares genre frequency through horizontal bar charts.

-Helps understand the variety of content available on Netflix.

**F. Total Movies & TV Shows by Year**
An area chart illustrates the distribution of movies and television shows added to Netflix across different years.
The visualization distinguishes between movies and TV shows using separate colored areas.
Key analysis:
-Examines changes in content additions over time.
-Compares movie and TV show addition patterns.
-Highlights the growth in content additions during the later years shown in the dataset.
-Identifies the peak content addition period in the displayed timeline.

**G. Content Details and Description**
The dashboard includes a dedicated section for displaying detailed information about a selected Netflix title.
It provides:
-Title-specific genre information.
-Content description.
-Release year.
-Content rating.
-Duration.
-Date added.
This section improves the dashboard's usability by combining analytical visualizations with detailed content exploration.

**5. Key Insights from the Dashboard**
Based on the visualizations:
-Movies represent approximately 68.42% of the analyzed Netflix catalogue, while TV shows account for 31.58%.
-The dataset contains a larger representation of movies compared with television shows.
-TV-MA and TV-14 are among the most frequently represented content rating categories.
-Documentaries and Stand-Up Comedy are prominent categories in the Top 10 genre visualization.
-The geographical map highlights differences in Netflix content representation across countries.
-The year-wise visualization shows a noticeable increase in content additions during the later years, with 2019 appearing as the peak year in the displayed timeline.
